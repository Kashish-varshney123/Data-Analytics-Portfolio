# Aarogyam Speciality Hospital – Healthcare Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes hospital patient and billing data to understand patient distribution, treatment costs, and insurance-based trends. The goal is to provide a clear analytics solution that helps hospital management monitor operational and financial performance.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze patient inflow across different departments  
- Identify high-cost departments and treatment patterns  
- Compare Insurance vs Non-Insurance patient metrics  
- Track monthly trends in patient count and treatment costs  
- Support data-driven decision making through dashboards  

---

## Tools Used
- SQL
- Power BI

---

## Dataset
- 900+ patient and billing records  
- 6 hospital departments  
- Key fields include:
  - patient_id
  - admission_date
  - department
  - treatment_cost
  - insurance_type
  - age
  - gender  

---

## Key KPIs & Analysis
- Total Patients  
- Average Cost per Patient  
- Department-wise Patient Count  
- Department-wise Treatment Cost  
- Insurance vs Non-Insurance Patients  
- Monthly Patient and Cost Trends  
- Identification of High-Cost Departments  

---

## Dashboard Features
- Department-wise performance overview  
- Insurance vs Non-Insurance comparison  
- Monthly patient inflow and treatment cost trends  
- Interactive filters for department and time period  

---

## Key Insights
- Identified departments contributing the highest treatment costs  
- Observed cost differences between insured and non-insured patients  
- Detected monthly variations in patient inflow and expenses
