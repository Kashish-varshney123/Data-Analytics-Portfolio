## 📁 Projects

### 📡 Telco Customer Churn Analysis
- Analyzed 7,043 customer records to identify why customers leave a telecom company
- Used SQL for querying, Python for data cleaning & visualization, Power BI for dashboard
- **Key Insights:**
  - 26.54% overall churn rate — 1 in 4 customers leaving
  - Month-to-month contract customers churn at 42.71% vs 2-year at 2.85%
  - New customers (0–12 months) are at highest risk — 47.68% churn rate
  - Electronic check users churn 3x more than auto-pay users
